An ordinary, unedited photograph taken inside a stream-cut ravine high in the Alps on a dull overcast day. It must be indistinguishable from a real photo a hiker took with a full-frame mirrorless camera and a 24mm lens, handheld, f/8, ISO 200, straight out of camera with a neutral colour profile. A plain record of a real place, not a showpiece.

Viewpoint: crouched in the bed of the ravine, looking straight up it. The framing is casual and a little off-centre; nothing looks arranged.

The ravine is the subject, and it is obviously the work of water. Over thousands of years the stream has sawn a steep V-shaped trench, five to eight metres deep and a few metres wide at the bottom, down through the mountainside. Its walls are bare, water-worn bedrock: dark slate and schist, smoothed and undercut near the bottom, with scour marks, potholes and a clear high-water line of pale silt, and loose scree and soil higher up. The bed is a chute of dull grey and brown cobbles and gravel of very uneven sizes with a modest stream of grey, silty meltwater running down the middle. The trench climbs away from the camera in a slight curve and, thirty to forty metres up, narrows to a dark rock slot with a small step where the water drops in. Everything the stream touches leads to that slot.

Above the rim of the ravine: patchy olive-brown alpine grass, scree, one or two dirty remnants of old snow, and beyond them a grey headwall with a small debris-covered glacier half hidden in low, flat cloud. Plain pale grey overcast sky.

How it looks: soft flat daylight with weak shadows and low contrast, the bottom of the ravine a little darker than the rim. Muted, slightly desaturated natural colour - greys, browns, olive, cold off-white. Haze that makes the distance paler. The brightest part of the sky is slightly clipped. A little fine sensor noise, slight softness towards the corners, real depth of field with the nearest stones a touch out of focus. Natural, unprocessed detail, never crisp or crunchy.

Avoid everything that makes an image look generated or retouched: no HDR look, no sunrise, sunset or golden light, no sun in frame, no light rays, no glowing mist, no orange and teal colour grade, no saturated colours, no dramatic clouds, no over-sharpening, no perfectly smooth round pebbles, no symmetry, no epic composition. Not a painting, not a 3D render, not concept art.

No people, no animals, no buildings, no paths, no text, no watermark, no border.
